YourNest is a website template built for people who want a beautiful personal site without learning to code.

## What it does

- **Write in markdown** — open a file, type your thoughts, save. That's publishing.
- **Organize naturally** — folders for blog posts, projects, creative writing. Put things where they make sense.
- **Look good by default** — a clean, responsive design that works on every screen size. Dark mode included.
- **Deploy automatically** — push to GitHub, your site updates. No servers to manage.

## Who it's for

Writers, artists, makers, freelancers — anyone who has things to share and wants a home for them on the internet. If you can write an email, you can use YourNest.

## How it works

1. Get the template
2. Open it in your editor with Claude (or your AI assistant of choice)
3. Tell Claude about yourself and what you want
4. Watch your site take shape

The AI collaboration isn't a gimmick — it's the core experience. The template includes a `CLAUDE.md` file that gives your assistant full context about how everything works, so the conversation starts productive.

## Philosophy

Your website should be yours. Not locked into a platform. Not dependent on a monthly subscription to exist. Not cluttered with someone else's branding.

YourNest gives you a site that lives on your machine, deploys from your GitHub, and belongs entirely to you. Fork it, change it, make it weird. It's your nest.
